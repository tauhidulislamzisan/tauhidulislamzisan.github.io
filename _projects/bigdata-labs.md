A series of lab reports for Fundamentals of Big Data Technology, each with code, figures and discussion.

- **Locality Sensitive Hashing:** shingling, MinHash signatures and banded hashing to find candidate similar sentences, plus a plot of candidate-pair probability against similarity for different band and row settings.
- **CURE clustering:** ran a Python CURE implementation on three 2D datasets (3clus, Jain, R15), scored with V-measure (NMI) and plotted the clusters.
- **Market basket analysis:** Apriori and association rules (mlxtend) on a grocery transactions dataset, sorted by support, confidence and lift.
