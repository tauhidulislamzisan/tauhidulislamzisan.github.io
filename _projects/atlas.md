Lead developer of ATLAS (Atlas Sweden AB / Atlas Group), a Test Management System for planning, execution, analysis and reporting. It has four modules (Planner, Live, Analysis and Reports) and includes Hermes, an in-execution communication feature for talking with participants during test execution.

- **Stack:** FastAPI, PostgreSQL, SQLAlchemy/Alembic, Celery, Redis, React/TypeScript with Vite; desktop functionality with Tauri.
- **Delivery:** containerised deployment with Docker and Docker Compose; review and merge of changes, including database migrations and shared components.
- **AI-assisted workflows:** repository-guided AI-assisted development workflows for non-engineering contributors, and controlled LLM tool-call integrations in the product.
