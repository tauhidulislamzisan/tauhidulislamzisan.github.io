Three lab reports for Compiler Principle and Technology, written in C (and JavaScript for the grammar-set tool).

- **Lexical analysis:** identifier and constant recognition derived from regular expressions through an NFA to a DFA, then implemented as a DFA-driven scanner.
- **Top-down parsing:** recursive-descent parsers for the grammar A → (A)A | ε and for a standard arithmetic-expression grammar.
- **FIRST and FOLLOW sets:** computation of FIRST and FOLLOW sets for context-free grammars, tested on several small grammars.
