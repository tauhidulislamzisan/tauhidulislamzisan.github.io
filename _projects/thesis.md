**When:** June 2025 · **Grade:** 89/100 · **Supervisor:** Dr. Wenjun Li · **Institution:** Changsha University of Science and Technology

**Stack:** Django, Random Forest (scikit-learn), role-based access control.

A Django school-management prototype with role-based workflows for administrators, students and lecturers, plus a Random Forest component for student-score prediction. The system was adapted from open-source foundations.

**Evaluation notes**
- This is a systems prototype, not a high-accuracy forecaster. Predicting exact scores is a many-class target and performed poorly in the archived experiments.
- Reformulating the target (for example score bands or regression) is the main piece of future work.
