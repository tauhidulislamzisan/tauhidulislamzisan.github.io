Co-founder of [Flash DSM](https://flashdsm.com), a design-system product that encodes design tokens and component rules as a machine-readable design genome so AI-assisted UI generation stays consistent.

- **Stack:** TypeScript monorepo with a Vite/React editor, FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL and Docker.
- **Scope:** MCP-oriented interfaces, editor/plugin integrations and design-token exports for AI coding tools.
