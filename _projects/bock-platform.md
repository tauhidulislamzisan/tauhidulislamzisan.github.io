Developer on the web platform for Bock Lighting at Cocoon, from an earlier Gatsby site to a headless stack with a Strapi content backend, a Next.js storefront and a Flask PDF service. It complements the 3D product builder entry.

- **Stack:** Gatsby then Next.js (React, Material UI, three.js), Strapi with custom admin plugins, Flask, Algolia search, Auth0, with hosting and storage on AWS and Google Cloud.
- **Content backend:** Strapi content types and APIs for products, projects and site settings, a product-builder API, batch search indexing, and custom admin plugins for building parts, logic rules and spec sheets.
- **Frontend and documents:** search-driven product pages, interactive 3D views, and PDF spec-sheet generation through a Flask service.
