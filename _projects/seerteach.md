Work at Guangdong Yu Jia Information Technology Co., Ltd. on the SeerTeach AI interactive learning platform.

- OCR on images of text, embedding-based retrieval and LLM feedback, served through FastAPI and vLLM.
- Multimodal correction flows combining OCR, text-to-speech and Transformer-based LLMs for automated feedback and content understanding.
- Prepared and curated educational materials from textbook images for model-adaptation workflows.
