**When:** August to November 2025 · **Role:** AI and Computer Engineering Specialist (onsite) · **Organisation:** Guangdong Yu Jia Information Technology Co., Ltd.

**Stack:** FastAPI, vLLM, embeddings and vector search, OCR, text-to-speech, Transformer-based LLMs.

**What it does:** OCR of textbook and handwritten images, then embedding retrieval, then LLM feedback, with text-to-speech, on the SeerTeach AI interactive learning platform.

**My role**
- Implemented embedding-based vector search pipelines and integrated LLM inference through FastAPI and vLLM.
- Built multimodal correction flows combining OCR, text-to-speech and Transformer-based LLMs for automated feedback and content understanding.
- Prepared and curated educational materials from textbook images for model-adaptation workflows.

This was integration and serving work. I did not train base models, and I make no claims about fine-tuning gains.
