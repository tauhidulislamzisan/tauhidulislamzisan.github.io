I am Tauhidul Islam Zisan, a Bangladeshi computer scientist and AI / full-stack engineer. I earned a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Changsha University of Science and Technology (2021–2025), and I build educational and product systems that integrate large language models, retrieval and OCR.

I currently lead development of [ATLAS](/projects/), a test management platform, and co-build [Flash DSM](https://flashdsm.com), a design-system product for AI-assisted UI generation. Previously I worked on a multimodal educational AI platform (OCR, embedding retrieval and LLM feedback served through FastAPI and vLLM), ranking pipelines for GoaldenView, and client products for Cocoon.

## Research interests

- Large language model training, adaptation and fine-tuning
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)
- Document-grounded and multimodal educational AI (OCR, vision-language, speech)

These are the directions I want to study in a master's programme; they are interests rather than a record of completed research. My one publication so far is on [AI and big data in auditing](/publications/) (2024).

You can find my [CV](/cv/), [projects](/projects/) and contact links on this site.
